Practical creator tips

Instagram growth strategies

Use these organic methods to learn what your audience responds to. Results vary and are never guaranteed.

01 · Profile

Make your profile clear

Use a recognisable profile image, a specific bio, a simple value proposition and a clear next step for visitors.

02 · Reels

Hook viewers quickly

Start with a useful question, result or moment. Keep the video focused, readable and relevant to your target audience.

03 · Consistency

Build a repeatable schedule

Choose a realistic posting rhythm and repeat content formats that are helpful to your audience.

04 · Engagement

Invite real conversation

Ask relevant questions, reply thoughtfully and collaborate with creators whose audiences overlap with yours.

05 · Analytics

Measure meaningful signals

Review watch time, saves, shares, profile visits and audience retention—not just follower count.

06 · Trust

Keep growth authentic

Avoid fake engagement, spam and misleading claims. Follow Instagram's current rules and disclose paid promotions where required.
